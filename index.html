Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

Terms of Service

“もしもシリーズ Uploader” (the “App”) is a private tool used only by the operator of the もしもシリーズ short-video series. It sends short science videos that the operator produced to the operator’s own TikTok account as drafts, using TikTok’s Content Posting API. The operator reviews each draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to publish it.

Who can use the App

The App is not offered to the public. It is used only with the operator’s own TikTok account, which the operator authorizes through TikTok Login Kit.

Content

The App only uploads videos created by the operator. The operator is responsible for the content and for following TikTok’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The App never publishes a video without the operator’s action in the TikTok app.

Availability and changes

The App is provided as is, without warranty. The operator may change or stop the App at any time and will update this page when the terms change.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the contact information on the YouTube channel above.

Privacy Policy

What the App accesses

  • The operator’s TikTok account identifier (open_id) and an access token, received through TikTok Login Kit after the operator authorizes the App.
  • The video.upload permission, used only to send the operator’s own videos to the operator’s TikTok inbox as drafts.

What the App does not do

  • It does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • It does not read messages, followers, comments or analytics.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Storage and retention

Access and refresh tokens are stored only as encrypted secrets in the operator’s private automation environment and are used only to upload drafts. The operator can revoke access at any time in TikTok’s settings (Security and permissions → Apps), after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Changes and contact

This policy is updated on this page when the App changes. Questions can be sent through the contact information on the YouTube channel above.
